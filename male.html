How to read this

Rates, not headcounts

A value of 1,000 represents 1,000 suspects per 10,000 residents in that nationality, sex and age category under the source's definition.

Finland is a reference

The Finnish-nationality bar is highlighted in amber. Regional and foreign-country totals are kept outside the ranking.

Age composition matters

These are not age-standardized rates. Compare age-specific groups for a more like-for-like view.

Interpret cautiously

Suspected does not mean convicted. No group population sizes or uncertainty estimates are included, and small populations can produce volatile rates.

Offence categories overlap

Some categories are parent totals containing subcategories; never add category rates together.

Data scope

Permanent residents of Finland, males, 2023–2025. Historical nationality labels are retained as provided.
